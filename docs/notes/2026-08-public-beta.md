# August 2026 — public beta note

The Agentic Payments Index is now in public beta. It begins with MPP and x402
because both expose public payment aggregates and service-level activity that
can be normalized into a comparable view. The product remains intentionally
quiet while a small group tests its definitions, questions, and usability.

The most important product rule is that protocol activity is not automatically
agent activity. Payer and recipient counts are identities reported by a source,
not verified people, companies, or autonomous agents. Every view carries that
boundary, and unsupported cohort or attribution questions return the evidence
that is missing instead of a fabricated answer.

Current totals are raw observed activity. Testing, duplicates, internal traffic,
and unresolved counterparties may remain. Quality-adjusted estimates will only
be published after direct history is backfilled, rules are public, results are
reconciled, and confidence ranges can travel with the estimate.

The next work is source reconciliation, direct-chain backfill, and broader
coverage research. Corrections, sources, and methodology proposals are welcome
through GitHub; the goal is for every contribution to improve both the data and
the relationship between the people building this market.
